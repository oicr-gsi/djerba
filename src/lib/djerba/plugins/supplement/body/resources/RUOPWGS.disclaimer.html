This assay is provided for Research Use Only (RUO). Analytical performance characteristics, including sensitivity, specificity, and limit of detection, have not been established on this platform. RUO reports are released as is, without review by our clinical interpretation team, using default pipeline settings for generation of reported metrics. Results generated using this workflow should be considered exploratory, and recipients are encouraged to evaluate findings in the context of other available data.
Minimal Residual Disease testing by Plasma whole genome sequencing encompasses all genomic regions in a specimen. Gene annotation is not performed in this assay and variants are not classified (e.g. by pathogeneity).
This test was developed/implemented by OICR Genomics. It has not been cleared or approved by the US Food and Drug Administration.
