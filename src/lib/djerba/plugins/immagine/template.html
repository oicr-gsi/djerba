Definitions

High-Risk Multiple Myeloma: Defined by the presence of any one of the following: chromosome 17p deletion, TP53 mutation, or biallelic deletion of 1p32; or any two of the following: t(4;14), t(14;16), or t(14;20); gain or amplification of chromosome 1q; or monoallelic deletion of 1p32.

TP53 mutation: Defined by an SNV or CNV in TP53. SNVs must pass all filtering criteria (PASS, VAF ≥10%, etc.), but are not required to be classified as oncogenic by OncoKB. CNVs include deletions, gains, and amplifications.

Immunoglobulin Rearrangements: Characterized using IgCaller, a Python program designed to identify immunoglobulin gene rearrangements and oncogenic translocations in lymphoid neoplasms. Calls are further filtered using a custom R script and retained if IGCaller_Score ≥100. Translocations t(4;14), t(14;16), and t(14;20) used in high-risk myeloma classification are determined from IgCaller output.

Cytogenetic abnormalities: Characterized using PURPLE-derived copy-number estimates. Chromosomal copy-number alterations are classified according to disease-specific criteria, using absolute copy-number thresholds and assuming a diploid-centred baseline rather than adjusting thresholds for estimated tumour ploidy. Interpretation should be approached with caution in samples with an aneuploid genomic baseline, as absolute copy-number thresholds may not accurately reflect chromosome-level gains or losses relative to the tumour's baseline ploidy.

Monosomy chromosomes 13, 14, and 17: Defined as >80% of the respective chromosome having TCN <1.2, with MACN = 0 for <50% of that span.

Chromosome 1p deletion: Defined as >80% of chromosome 1p having TCN ≤1.5 and median MACN ≤0.5.

Chromosome 17p deletion: Defined as >80% of the region having TCN ≤1.2, with MACN = 0 for <50% of that span.

Gain or amplification of chromosome 1q: Defined as ≥50% of chromosome 1q having TCN ≥3.

Hyperdiploidy: Defined by gains (TCN >3) in ≥4 canonical hyperdiploid chromosomes (3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 15, 19, and 21), with a gain requiring CN ≥3 across at least 80% of the chromosome.

Biallelic deletion of 1p32: Defined as ≥50% of the 1p32 region having TCN ≤1 and MACN = 0.

Monoallelic deletion of 1p32: Defined as ≥50% of the 1p32 region having 1 ≤ TCN ≤1.5 and MACN = 0.

APOBEC Mutational Signatures: Assessed using HRDetect. COSMIC SBS2 and SBS13 signatures are considered detected when their respective scores are >0.
